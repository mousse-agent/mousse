# Native Plus local trial

I added this feature on the local PR45 + PR52 integration baseline `4dafc66589a3784d6bafdc6489366ef21d590cfc`. I keep that baseline separate from the feature commit. This trial is not production qualification, and I have not pushed the combined integration branch.

Plus remains optional. Existing direct/self-hosted transports keep their wire and configuration. The distinct `plus-relay` carrier uses the independently selected audience ending in `/v1/net/relay`, signed outer admission, and unchanged pinned inner Net TLS and endpoint authorization. The six pinned wrapper files and their license are in `src/mms/net/plus/wire/README.md`.

A profile must enable Net and protect/unlock its keys before connecting Plus. In Devices, enter the Plus API origin, choose explicit root binding for an authority profile, and approve the bounded code in the browser. The daemon retains PKCE and polling secrets in memory, validates the returned installation/audience, signs with its node, and consumes the short grant for binding/registration. The renderer receives only approval details and safe status. Root binding separately signs with the actual unlocked root; a member or account session cannot substitute for root proof.

The CLI supports `net plus login --configuration-file <public-json> [--bind-root]`, then `net plus login finish <id>`. Configuration includes `apiOrigin`, `audience`, `installationId`, and `gatewayId`. The one-shot bootstrap alternatives are `net plus bind --configuration-file <json-with-accountId> --account-token-file <private-file>` and `net plus connect --account-token-file <private-file>`. I reject token arguments, symlinks, nonregular or group/world-accessible credential files. The account grant/bearer is never persisted. The durable connector and exact pending mutation retry data are held in the profile's encrypted keystore.

I renew the connector lease while running and recover an expired lease using fresh node proof before startup/unlock activation. A hosted recovery failure records an error and allows local transport startup. `net plus renew` provides explicit retry; failed background renewal stops until explicit recovery. `net plus disconnect` disables the carrier and revokes the registration. Configuration changes require disconnecting first.

Sponsored `mj1` device enrollment uses a signed `plus-relay` rendezvous. A prepared device proves its key through a narrowly scoped `rendezvous_<ticket-hash>` outer registration marker; the actual enrollment and root-signed delegation remain inner Net operations. The new device does not receive a general hosted account registration or root private key. Hosted human Space invitations carry a separate issuer-signed outer discovery statement bound to the exact inner authorization and descriptor. A protected account-connected participant dials with its own registration and the opaque ticket; after endpoint membership commits, the host automatically approves the source route. The relay never receives the inner invitation or membership authority. The outer ticket remains encrypted in the join journal keystore and stays out of membership history. Hosted invitations admit one exact source device, expire for outer discovery within ten minutes, and require a separate invitation per participant. `net plus allow <source-node>` remains an advanced fallback. Control-plane rendezvous purpose is signed explicitly: only enrollment permits the prepared-node marker; Space requires a current account registration.

I verified the protected profile/credential lifecycle and owner-framed disabled behavior; CLI private-file/symlink checks; browser begin/poll/exchange against the actual Plus login/control services; an actual protected blank-device join against the hosted control policy/gateway; a two-account hosted Space invitation/join, automatic route consent, regular reconnect and public message delivery; and two parallel pinned TLS connections with exporter agreement, wrong-pin refusal, and more than 900 KB of valid mux parts per connection fragmented into bounded wrapper frames. The cross-repository checks require `MOUSSE_PLUS_ROOT` pointing at a built local Plus checkout. I also passed both native/renderer typechecks and built the emitted CLI. I did not run a full suite, packaged GUI, macOS/Windows keystore checks, hosted deployment, or a long-running renewal/rollback soak.

Private incremental-write R2 remains the release gate from the alignment plan. Packaging, online identity-provider approval, production deployment, and durable multi-node lifecycle qualification remain separate gates. The GUI form and approval timeout are implemented but were not exercised in a live Electron window.
